Notes on backups that actually restore

2 November 2025

A backup you have never restored from is a hypothesis, not a backup. Test the restore on a schedule or accept that you do not have one.

The failures I have actually hit were never the copy step. They were a key nobody could find, an archive that unpacked into the wrong root, and one memorable case where the job had been writing zero-byte files nightly for five months while reporting success.

That last one is the argument for checking size and content rather than exit status. A job that cannot fail is not reliable. It is unmonitored.
