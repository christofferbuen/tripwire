Reading old code

19 July 2025

Most of what looks like bad judgement in an old codebase is a constraint you cannot see any more.

The strange retry loop was a vendor who rate-limited without saying so. The duplicated model was two teams on different release trains. The function nobody dares touch is holding up a report someone reads monthly.

Worth assuming the author was as capable as you and had worse information. It makes the archaeology faster and the rewrite smaller.
